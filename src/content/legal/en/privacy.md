## No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies. It loads no analytics, no advertising, no tag manager, no social
widgets and no embedded video. Every file it needs — including the fonts — is served from this
domain, so opening a page sends your IP address to no one but our own server.

The one thing stored on your device is a single browser preference, `grid-visible`, written only
when you press the grid button in the header. It records whether you want the layout grid drawn,
never leaves your browser, and can be removed by clearing site data.

The search is built into the page: it downloads an index and runs in your browser. What you type is
never sent anywhere.

## What the server records

Our hosting provider records ordinary web-server logs — IP address, time, requested address, user
agent — which are needed to serve the site and to detect abuse. The legal basis is our legitimate
interest in operating and securing the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). They are not used to profile
anyone.

These logs belong to the running server instance: they are discarded whenever the site is
redeployed, which happens whenever the site changes.

The site is served from Amsterdam, the Netherlands, by Railway, which acts as our processor. The
data stays inside the European Union.

## People named on this site

The team page publishes professional information about members of Laptenok Labs, and the CV names
academic supervisors, opponents and former employers as a matter of professional record. The basis
is our legitimate interest in describing our work accurately.

## Your rights

You may ask for access to your personal data, for its correction or erasure, for restriction of
processing, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to
customercare@laptenoklabs.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal
Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, uoou.gov.cz).
