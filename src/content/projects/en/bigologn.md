**O(log n)** is the learning platform of Laptenok Labs: a place to distribute the software
engineering courses we produce ourselves, and to run the teaching that sits behind the figures on
the front page — computer networks, operating systems, databases, algorithms and data structures,
parallel computing, probability theory and discrete mathematics.

It grew out of the classes we have been giving since 2009, and it is designed around the way
engineers actually learn: work through real systems, get read by someone who has built them, and
come back with better questions.
